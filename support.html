Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or pause audio?

Tap anywhere on the screen or tap the center play/pause button on the dock. You can also use your AirPods hardware controls, Lock Screen controls, or iOS Control Center to play and pause seamlessly.

Does Calmpal support background playback?

Yes. Calmpal continues playing high-fidelity ambient nature sounds even when you lock your iPhone screen or switch to another app.

How do I adjust the sleep timer?

Tap the pencil icon on the dock to enter Edit Mode. Drag the measuring lines up or down to set your desired countdown (1 minute up to 4 hours), then tap the checkmark to confirm.

How do I change the ambient sound?

Tap the music note icon on the dock to open the Relaxing Sounds library, or tap the left (‹) and right (›) arrows to quickly skip between soundscapes.

What is Zen Mode?

Tap the eye icon at the very top of your screen to hide all interface elements for a distraction-free, pure nature immersion.
