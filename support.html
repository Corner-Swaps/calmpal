About Calmpal

Calmpal is a focused, high-fidelity ambient nature sounds and sleep timer application designed for iOS. Engineered for relaxation, meditation, deep work, and healthy sleep routines, Calmpal provides high-definition audio landscapes with seamlessly looped buffers and custom sleep countdown timers.

Core application capabilities include:

  • 21 high-definition atmospheric sound profiles including Gentle Rain, Forest River, Peaceful Ocean Waves, Cozy Campfire, Crickets Night, Sacred Temple, and Heavy Downpour.
  • Customizable sleep timer with fluid wave duration control ranging from 1 minute to 4 hours.
  • Distraction-free Zen immersion mode to remove interface elements and display full photographic backdrops.
  • Seamless background audio playback with interruption recovery for incoming calls and system alarms.
  • Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and hardware headphone playback controls via Apple Remote Command Center.
  • 100% on-device local execution with zero cloud telemetry, no account requirements, and complete offline functionality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or pause audio playback?

Tap anywhere on the main screen or tap the center play/pause button on the bottom control dock. You can also use your AirPods hardware press controls, Apple Watch media controls, Lock Screen player, or the iOS Control Center.

Does Calmpal play audio in the background?

Yes. Calmpal is configured with background audio playback capabilities. Ambient soundscapes will continue playing seamlessly when you lock your iPhone screen or navigate to other applications.

How do I configure the sleep timer?

Tap the pencil icon on the bottom dock to enter Edit Mode. Drag the fluid wave measuring lines vertically to set your desired countdown duration, then tap the checkmark icon to save and start the countdown.

How do I select different soundscapes?

Tap the music note icon on the dock to open the full Sound Library sheet, or tap the left and right directional arrows to immediately step to adjacent soundscapes.

What is Zen Mode?

Zen Mode is activated by tapping the eye icon at the top of the interface. It hides all buttons, timers, and text so you can enjoy an unobstructed, serene nature visual while listening to your selected ambient sound.

Does Calmpal require an internet connection?

No. All 21 high-definition audio recordings and graphical assets are bundled directly within the app binary. Calmpal works completely offline in Airplane Mode with zero internet access required.

Does Calmpal drain battery in the background?

Calmpal is built using native AVFoundation audio engine buffers, which are optimized by iOS to consume minimal CPU cycles and preserve battery life during overnight sleep sessions.
