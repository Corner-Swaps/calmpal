Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or pause audio?

Tap anywhere on the main circular timer screen or tap the center play/pause dock button. You can also use your AirPods hardware controls, Lock Screen widget, or iOS Control Center to play and pause seamlessly.

Does Calmpal support background audio playback?

Yes. Calmpal is engineered with full iOS background audio capabilities (`UIBackgroundModes: audio`). It continues playing your selected ambient nature soundscape when you lock your iPhone screen or switch to another app.

How do I adjust the sleep timer?

Tap the pencil icon on the bottom dock to enter Edit Mode. Drag the fluid wave measuring lines up or down to set your desired countdown (from 1 minute up to 4 hours), then tap the checkmark button to confirm.

How do I switch ambient soundscapes?

Tap the music note icon on the dock to open the full Relaxing Sounds library, or use the left (‹) and right (›) arrows to quickly skip to adjacent atmospheric recordings.

What is Zen Mode?

Tap the subtle eye icon at the very top of your screen to hide all interface elements, clock rings, and buttons for a distraction-free, pure photographic nature immersion.
