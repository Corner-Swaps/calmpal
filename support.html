Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start or pause audio?

Tap the central circle on your screen to play or pause your ambient soundscape. You can also use your AirPods hardware controls or iOS Control Center to play and pause seamlessly.

Does Calmpal support background playback?

Yes. Calmpal continues playing high-fidelity ambient sounds even when you switch to another app or lock your iPhone screen.

How do I add or remove sounds from my grounding circle?

Tap the plus button at the bottom of your screen to open the Sound Library and add new sounds. Tap the edit button to remove sounds from your custom layout.

How do I customize the app theme?

Tap the theme button at the top right corner of the screen to cycle through rich dark mode color themes.

Privacy & Data

Calmpal is built with privacy first. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data, listening history, or location information.
