1. Executive Summary & Zero-Sale Guarantee

Calmpal is an atmospheric soundscape and sleep timer application engineered with a privacy-first architecture. We guarantee unconditionally:

  • Zero Data Selling: We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, session recordings, or listening habits to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties under CCPA/CPRA § 1798.120 and GDPR Article 21.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy cross-site tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • On-Device Processing: Sleep timer countdowns, audio mixing, and volume settings are processed locally in your browser or native device memory.
